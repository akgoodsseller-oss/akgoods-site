Questions doctors ask

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a new phone number?

Yes — a new SIM for the clinic's WhatsApp line. Your current number stays exactly as it is. The new number becomes your booking line, and we do the set-up; you only need the SIM for a one-time verification code.

Does it replace my current software?

No. It works alongside whatever you use today. Many clinics keep their records system and use AK Goods for everything that happens on WhatsApp.

What if a patient describes an emergency?

The assistant recognises emergency descriptions — such as chest pain, difficulty breathing or stroke signs — and tells the patient to call 112 immediately, before anything else. The conversation is flagged for the doctor.

Will it give medical advice?

No. It answers practical questions — timings, fees, location, preparation — and books appointments. Medical questions are directed to the doctor.

Can patients write in Hindi?

Yes. Patients can write in Hindi, in English, or a mix of both, the way people actually message.

Where is my patients' data kept?

Each clinic's records are kept separately and shared only with that clinic. See our privacy policy for exactly which services are involved.

What happens after the pilot?

If it works for you, it continues at ₹2,999 a month. If not, we switch it off — no contract to break.
